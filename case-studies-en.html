Demand Planning & S&OP February 2026

Demand Planning: Energy Protein

Strategic analysis and production plan modeling following an unexpected global demand surge.

Case Study Background

Energy Protein is a company specializing in protein energy drinks distributed through two retailers: Auchan (larger volume driver due to store size) and Casino.

The company introduced a new Strawberry flavor, testing it gradually through Auchan. An influencer discovered the product, shared it with her community, and caused an instant viral surge that exhausted store stocks. Auchan subsequently submitted an urgent purchase order for 1,000 units.

Evaluated Scenarios

1. Prudent Scenario
Hypothesis based on maintaining moderate volume growth to limit exposure to a rapid decline in social media hype.
2. Growth Scenario
Hypothesis of progressively scaling up Strawberry flavor production to support market adoption.

Exercise Conclusions Table

Exercise results and strategic recommendations:

Element Analysis
Forecast M+1: Selected Scenario For this first month, the Growth scenario was selected. The enthusiasm around the Strawberry flavor is brand new, making it a sound strategy to produce more incrementally while closely monitoring market feedback.
Forecast M+2: Selected Scenario Continuing the M+1 trend, gradually increasing Strawberry production while keeping other flavors constant allows consumers to become familiar with it, bringing its volume closer to established flavors over time.
Forecast M+3: Selected Scenario Following the same momentum as M+1 and M+2, progressively scaling production ensures product availability while aligning with demand patterns.
Priority Flavor At this stage, no single priority flavor can be established: sales history shows that Chocolate and Vanilla are equally popular (with a subtle late lead for Chocolate). As Strawberry was just introduced, consumption trends must be monitored before defining a lead flavor—not to prioritize it exclusively, but to ensure timely replenishment and avoid stockouts.
Main Risk The primary risk is that the social media hype fades as quickly as it arrived, as social trends are inherently temporary.
Recommended Action Avoid relying solely on this demand peak, given total production capacity is capped at 900 units across all sales points. In the short term, negotiate with Auchan to stagger or reduce their 1,000-unit order. In the longer term, a phased capacity expansion can be evaluated if demand remains high.
Should Production Capacity Be Permanently Increased? Why? No, not for now. Current capacity should not be expanded immediately. Based on forecasts, required production remains within the 900-unit ceiling, offering enough margin for short-term growth. The decision should be re-evaluated only if demand consistently exceeds available capacity.
How to Manage the Influencer Effect? Compare pre- and post-influencer sales trends to measure true impact. If sustained growth is observed over several months, it will serve as a reliable signal to revise forecasts and adjust production permanently.

Downstream Oil Analytics January 2026

MF Energy: Oil Distribution & Inventory Management

A simulated supply chain case study designed to demonstrate how data can be used to monitor inventory, analyze consumption, and support replenishment decisions across a network of service stations.

Project Overview & Context

MF Energies is a simulated petroleum distribution company operating a network of service stations. The objective is to build a data-driven approach to monitor station inventory, understand consumption patterns, and anticipate replenishment needs while avoiding unnecessary overstocking.

Business Problem

How can MF Energies use its operational data to:

Inventory Monitoring
Monitor inventory levels across stations.
Risk Identification
Identify stations at risk of stockout.
Consumption Analysis
Analyze consumption patterns and evaluate delivery performance.

Data Analysis Approach (3 Steps)

1. Data Preparation in Excel
Structuring and preparing operational data for stations, inventory, consumption, and deliveries.
2. Power Query ETL
Cleaning, checking, and preparing 4 datasets (Stations, Stock, Consumption, Delivery).
3. Power BI Visualization
Developing a Power BI dashboard to monitor key inventory and replenishment indicators.

Data Structure

Station Master Data
Stock Inventory Data
Consumption Data
Delivery Data

Power BI Dashboard Preview

Key Findings & Supply Chain Insights

1. Gasoil Strain
Gasoil is the main point of tension in the network (335,000 L consumed; 34% more than Essence and double Super). As of Jan 31, it is the only product on replenishment alert across all 5 stations simultaneously.
2. Logistics Delays (ST004 & ST005)
The month's two unresolved deliveries both concern Gasoil at ST004 and ST005. Delays stem from process execution (processing time, truck availability) rather than carrier zone coverage.
3. Dual Replenishment Alert on ST001
ST001 (Dakar) is on alert for both Essence and Gasoil simultaneously, requiring a tailored monitoring approach rather than standard replenishment cadences.
Limitations of Analysis
This static diagnosis identifies current pressure points. Future turnover and coverage rate models will provide dynamic early-warning capabilities.
