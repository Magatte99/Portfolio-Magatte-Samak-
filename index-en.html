MF Energy: Supply Chain Analytics Case Study

Downstream Oil Distribution

Applied data analytics case study evaluating retail station fuel replenishment and inventory optimization.

  • Business Problem: Prioritize replenishment orders to prevent station stockouts while eliminating holding costs from overstocking.
  • Data Preparation (Excel): Structuring 4 core operational datasets (Station Master Data, Inventory, Consumption, Deliveries).
  • Data Transformation (Power Query): Data cleaning, anomaly checks, and data model relationship building.
  • Visualization & Monitoring (Power BI): Interactive dashboard model analyzing consumption patterns, carrier delivery SLA performance, and critical replenishment alerts.
Key Findings & Operational Insights
  • Diesel (Gasoil) - Critical Bottleneck: 335,000 L consumed during the period (+34% vs Unleaded, 2x Super). Diesel was the only product simultaneously triggering replenishment alerts across all 5 stations.
  • ST004 & ST005 Stations: Combined high Diesel consumption with recurring carrier delivery delays. Carrier route mapping confirmed issue was operational rather than geographic coverage.
  • ST001 Station: The only retail site requiring dual replenishment (Unleaded + Diesel simultaneously).
